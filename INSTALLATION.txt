Iconify Search for Word — installation
=========================================

Recommended antivirus-safe installation
---------------------------------------

This package contains no executable installer and makes no direct Windows
Registry changes.

1. Extract the complete ZIP.
2. Open Word and open or create a document.
3. Choose Home > Add-ins > More Add-ins.
4. Open My Add-ins.
5. Choose Manage My Add-ins > Upload My Add-in.
6. Browse to manifest.xml in this folder and choose Upload.
7. Choose Home > Iconify > Search Icons.

Depending on the Office build, “Upload My Add-in” can instead appear under
Advanced or More Settings in the Add-ins dialog.

Internet access is required while searching Iconify. The add-in interface is
hosted on GitHub Pages and the manifest uses HTTPS.

Removal
-------

Open Home > Add-ins > More Add-ins > My Add-ins. Open the menu for Iconify
Search and choose Remove.
