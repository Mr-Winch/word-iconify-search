Iconify Search for Word — installation
=========================================

Recommended installation
------------------------

1. Extract the complete ZIP.
2. Close every Word window.
3. Double-click Install Iconify Search for Word.cmd.
4. When installation finishes, close the installer.
5. Open Word yourself.
6. Choose Home > Iconify > Search Icons.

The installer does not launch Word. It copies manifest.xml to your local
profile and registers the add-in only for your Windows user. It does not use
PowerShell, download programs, or require administrator access.

Manual alternative
------------------

Open Word, then choose Home > Add-ins > More Add-ins > My Add-ins >
Manage My Add-ins > Upload My Add-in and select manifest.xml. Some builds put
this option under Advanced or More Settings.

Removal
-------

For an installation made with the CMD installer, close Word and run:
Uninstall Iconify Search for Word.cmd

For a manually uploaded add-in, remove Iconify Search from My Add-ins.
